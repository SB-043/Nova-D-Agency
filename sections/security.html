Security

Nova Digital is committed to maintaining the security, integrity, and resilience of our systems, projects, and digital infrastructure.

Our Security Commitment

Security is foundational to everything we build. Nova Digital follows secure development practices, principle-of-least-privilege design, and continuous risk evaluation across our systems and projects.

While no system can be guaranteed to be entirely free of risk, we take proactive steps to identify, mitigate, and respond to vulnerabilities responsibly.

Responsible Disclosure

We welcome responsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities affecting Nova Digital properties, tools, or infrastructure.

If you believe you have identified a security issue, please report it privately with sufficient detail to allow reproduction and analysis.

Safe Harbor

Nova Digital considers security research conducted in good faith and within responsible disclosure guidelines to be authorized.

We do not pursue legal action against researchers who:

  • Avoid privacy violations and data destruction
  • Do not exploit issues beyond proof-of-concept
  • Report findings promptly and responsibly

Scope

This policy applies to publicly accessible Nova Digital websites, tools, and demonstration systems.

Third-party services, dependencies, or user-operated environments are outside of our control and scope.

Reporting a Vulnerability

Please include the following when reporting:

  • Description of the vulnerability
  • Steps to reproduce
  • Potential impact
